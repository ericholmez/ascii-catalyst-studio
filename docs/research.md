# Research notes

- Xu, Zhang and Wong, *Structure-based ASCII Art*, SIGGRAPH 2010. Motivates line shape matching and alignment tolerance. The engine independently scores 4×8 alpha masks with soft overlap and an optional shift search. [Project and paper](https://ttwong12.github.io/papers/asciiart/asciiart.html)
- Xu et al., *ASCII Art Synthesis from Natural Photographs*, IEEE TVCG 2017. Structural abstraction and texture suppression inform the hybrid mode’s distinction between contours and smooth tones. [Publication record](https://research.monash.edu/en/publications/ascii-art-synthesis-from-natural-photographs/)
- Martin Krzywinski, *ASCII art*. Fixed-string and proportional examples inform Text Lab and positioned typography on the roadmap. [Artist’s reference](https://mk.bcgsc.ca/asciiart/)
- W3C documents the sRGB linearization and weighting offered by the engine. [Relative luminance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html)
- Unicode defines the Braille code points and dot patterns used by the subcell converter. [Braille chart](https://www.unicode.org/charts/PDF/U2800.pdf)
- The history overview provides context for the Index and historical export formats. [ASCII art overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ASCII_art)

Source material informed the design. The code, sample artwork and example grid fixtures were authored for this repository.
